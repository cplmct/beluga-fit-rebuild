## Content requirements

Use concise, original, practical coaching content.

Avoid:

- “Perfect form” claims.
- Claims that an exercise completely isolates a muscle.
- Guaranteed injury-prevention claims.
- Unsupported medical claims.
- Copying wording from websites.
- Treating normal individual variation as automatically incorrect.

Use adaptable language such as:

- “A useful starting position is…”
- “Choose a range of motion you can control comfortably.”
- “This version places relatively more emphasis on…”
- “Adjust the setup to your mobility, equipment, and goal.”

### Pull-ups

Cover:

- Hanging setup.
- Shoulder control.
- Pulling the body toward the bar.
- Controlled lowering.
- Grip options.
- Regressions such as assisted pull-ups and pulldowns.

### Chin-ups

Cover:

- Supinated or neutral grip.
- Shoulder and trunk position.
- Elbow drive.
- Controlled descent.
- How the grip changes the movement demand.

### Lat Pulldown

Cover:

- Seat and thigh-pad setup.
- Grip selection.
- Pulling toward the upper chest without excessive swinging.
- Controlled return.
- Avoiding pulling the bar behind the neck.

### Dumbbell Row

Cover:

- Supported or hinged setup.
- Stable shoulder and trunk position.
- Pulling toward the hip or lower ribs.
- Avoiding torso rotation.
- One-arm and chest-supported variations.

### T-Bar Row

Cover:

- Stance and hinge.
- Bracing.
- Load path.
- Elbow path.
- Controlling the eccentric.
- Reduced-range or chest-supported alternatives.

### Seated Cable Row

Cover:

- Upright but not rigid posture.
- Shoulder-blade movement without shrugging.
- Pulling the handle toward the torso.
- Controlled reach forward.
- Different grips and chest-supported alternatives.

### Face Pulls

Cover:

- Cable height and rope setup.
- Pulling toward the face or forehead.
- External-rotation-friendly finish.
- Light, controlled loading.
- Avoiding lumbar extension and excessive momentum.

### Hyperextensions

Cover:

- Hip hinge rather than forced spinal extension.
- Neutral or comfortable trunk position.
- Glute and hamstring contribution.
- Controlled range.
- Bodyweight, dumbbell, or reduced-range variations.

### Kettlebell Row

Cover:

- Supported or hinged stance.
- Keeping the kettlebell close.
- Pulling toward the hip.
- Avoiding rotation.
- Easier supported variation.

### Kettlebell Deadlift

Cover:

- Kettlebell position between or slightly in front of the feet.
- Hinge mechanics.
- Creating tension.
- Coordinated hip and knee extension.
- Keeping the kettlebell close.
- Elevated-start and sumo variations.

### Kettlebell Renegade Row

Cover:

- Stable plank or elevated setup.
- Feet width and anti-rotation.
- Rowing one kettlebell while maintaining trunk control.
- Regressions before floor-level execution.
- Avoiding excessive hip rotation.

### Kettlebell Good Morning

Cover:

- Safe kettlebell placement.
- Hip hinge.
- Soft knees.
- Keeping the spine controlled.
- Light loading and range limitations.
- Bodyweight and band alternatives.

### Typewriter Pull-ups

Cover:

- Required pull-up strength.
- Pulling high enough to move side to side.
- Controlled lateral travel.
- Shoulder comfort and range.
- Assisted, banded, or archer-pull-up regressions.

### Australian Pull-ups

Cover:

- Bar height and body angle.
- Straight trunk.
- Pulling chest toward the bar.
- Scaling difficulty by changing body angle.
- Feet-supported and elevated-foot variations.
