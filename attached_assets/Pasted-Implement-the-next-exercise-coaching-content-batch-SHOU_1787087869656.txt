## Exercise-specific requirements

### Overhead Press

Include:

- Standing setup.
- Grip and wrist position.
- Trunk bracing.
- Pressing path around the head.
- Controlled lowering.
- Seated, dumbbell, and landmine alternatives.

### Military Press

Treat this as a distinct, stricter barbell overhead-press variation.

Include:

- More constrained stance.
- Minimal leg drive.
- Braced trunk.
- Controlled bar path.
- Clear distinction from a push press.
- Appropriate regressions and dumbbell alternatives.

### Dumbbell Shoulder Press

Include:

- Seated or standing setup.
- Independent arm movement.
- Neutral and pronated grip options.
- Controlled lowering.
- Avoiding forced range or excessive back arch.

### Arnold Press

Include:

- Starting position with palms facing inward.
- Controlled rotation during the press.
- Avoiding forced shoulder rotation.
- Lighter loading and controlled tempo.
- Standard dumbbell press and landmine alternatives.

### Lateral Raises

Include:

- Light-to-moderate loading.
- Raising in a comfortable plane.
- Controlled top position.
- Avoiding momentum and excessive shrugging.
- Cable and leaning variations.

### Front Raises

Include:

- Controlled shoulder flexion.
- Dumbbell, plate, and cable options.
- Avoiding excessive trunk lean.
- Recognition that pressing movements may already train the front delts substantially.
- Range of motion based on comfort and control.

### Rear Delt Fly

Include:

- Supported or hinged setup.
- Scapular control.
- Moving the arms without turning it into a shrug or row.
- Dumbbell, cable, and reverse-pec-deck variations.

### Upright Row

Use cautious, non-absolute language.

Include:

- Comfortable grip width.
- Raising only through a pain-free, controlled range.
- No requirement to pull the elbows to a fixed height.
- Alternatives such as lateral raises and cable high pulls.
- Guidance to stop or modify if shoulder discomfort occurs.

### Shrugs

Include:

- Vertical shoulder elevation.
- Controlled pause and lowering.
- Avoiding shoulder rolling as a required technique.
- Barbell, dumbbell, and cable variations.
- Neck relaxation.

### Cable Lateral Raises

Include:

- Cable position.
- Stable stance.
- Smooth tension through the range.
- Avoiding trunk leaning and momentum.
- Single-arm and behind-the-body cable variations.

### Kettlebell Press

Include:

- Rack position.
- Wrist and forearm alignment.
- Bracing.
- Press path.
- Single-arm balance demands.
- Bottom-up or half-kneeling regressions where appropriate.

### Kettlebell Halo

Include:

- Light kettlebell selection.
- Controlled path around the head.
- Stable ribs and trunk.
- Comfortable shoulder range.
- Kneeling and plate alternatives.

### Kettlebell Clean and Press

Include:

- Clean setup.
- Keeping the kettlebell close.
- Quiet rack position.
- Strict press versus push-press distinction.
- Separate clean and press practice as a regression.
- Avoiding impact on the forearm.

### Kettlebell Front Raise

Include:

- Light loading.
- One- or two-arm options.
- Controlled shoulder flexion.
- Avoiding momentum and rib flare.
- Plate or cable alternatives.

### Handstand Push-ups

Treat this as an advanced movement.

Include:

- Prerequisite overhead and handstand control.
- Wall-assisted setup.
- Stable hand position.
- Controlled descent.
- Appropriate depth and range.
- Elevated pike push-up, partial-range, and dumbbell-press regressions.
- Do not imply that unsupported handstand push-ups are suitable for every user.
