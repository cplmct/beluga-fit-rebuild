## Image audit

For every image reference in the guidance data:

1. Confirm the referenced asset exists.
2. Confirm the path is correct for Expo/React Native static asset loading.
3. Confirm the asset is an approved exercise-specific image.
4. Confirm no `.jpg` or `.jpeg` references remain.
5. Confirm no image from one exercise is reused for another exercise unless that reuse is explicitly documented and appropriate.
6. Confirm text-only fallback phases do not include broken image references.

Report counts for:

- Entries with approved Start images.
- Entries with approved Finish images.
- Entries with approved Mid images, if any.
- Entries with text-only Start phases.
- Entries with text-only Mid phases.
- Entries with text-only Finish phases.
- Exercises with unresolved artwork.

The app must never render a broken image, empty image box, or missing local asset.

## Content-quality audit

Review all 112 entries for content quality.

For each entry, verify:

- Start guidance describes a plausible setup or starting position.
- Mid guidance describes the movement, control, alignment, and breathing where appropriate.
- Finish guidance describes a plausible end position or safe completion.
- Coaching tips are specific to the exercise.
- Descriptions are not identical generic text copied across unrelated exercises.
- Equipment-specific details are correct.
- Body position and movement direction are correct.
- High-risk or complex movements use cautious, clear wording.
- No medical claims or guaranteed outcomes are included.
- No unsafe absolute statements are included.
- No exercise is described as pain-free, risk-free, or appropriate for everyone.

Pay special attention to:

- Deadlift variations.
- Squat variations.
- Bench press variations.
- Overhead pressing.
- Kettlebell movements.
- Jumping exercises.
- Olympic-style or explosive movements.
- Pull-ups, muscle-ups, and other hanging movements.
- Planks and side planks.
- Single-leg exercises.
- Exercises with unusual or advanced names.

Report any entries that require manual review.
