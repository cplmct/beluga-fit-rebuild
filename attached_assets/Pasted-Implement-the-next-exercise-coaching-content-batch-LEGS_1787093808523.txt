## Exercise-specific requirements

### Front Squat

Include:

- Front-rack or suitable alternative grip.
- Upright trunk.
- Bracing.
- Controlled squat depth.
- Elbows and upper-back position.
- Cross-arm, goblet, and lighter-load regressions.

### Leg Press

Include:

- Seat and back-pad setup.
- Foot placement options.
- Controlled lowering.
- Avoiding excessive depth that causes the pelvis to lose contact.
- Avoiding forceful knee lockout.
- Single-leg and reduced-load variations.

### Leg Extension

Include:

- Machine alignment.
- Shin-pad position.
- Controlled knee extension and lowering.
- Comfortable range.
- Avoiding momentum.
- Single-leg and tempo variations.

### Leg Curl

Include:

- Machine alignment.
- Pad position.
- Controlled knee flexion and return.
- Avoiding hip lifting or excessive momentum.
- Seated, lying, and single-leg options.

### Romanian Deadlift

Include:

- Hip-hinge pattern.
- Soft knees.
- Load close to the body.
- Hamstring tension without forcing depth.
- Controlled return to standing.
- Dumbbell, kettlebell, and single-leg progressions.

### Lunges

Include:

- Step length and balance.
- Front-foot pressure.
- Controlled descent.
- Knee tracking.
- Forward, reverse, walking, and supported variations.

### Bulgarian Split Squat

Include:

- Rear-foot support height.
- Front-foot position.
- Balance and trunk control.
- Comfortable depth.
- Avoiding forced rear-leg loading.
- Supported and reduced-range regressions.

### Calf Raises

Include:

- Stable foot position.
- Full controllable ankle range.
- Pause at the top if useful.
- Controlled lowering.
- Standing, seated, single-leg, and supported variations.

### Leg Abduction

Include:

- Machine or band setup.
- Pelvis stability.
- Controlled outward movement.
- Avoiding trunk leaning.
- Range based on control and comfort.
- Standing, side-lying, and band variations.

### Kettlebell Goblet Squat

Include:

- Kettlebell held close.
- Stance and balance.
- Bracing.
- Controlled depth.
- Elbow and knee relationship without rigid universal rules.
- Box and tempo variations.

### Kettlebell Swing

Treat this as a ballistic hip-hinge movement.

Include:

- Hinge rather than squat-dominant setup.
- Bell path close to the body.
- Powerful hip extension.
- Relaxed but controlled arms.
- Letting the bell float rather than lifting it with the shoulders.
- Russian swing, dead-stop swing, and lighter-hinge regressions.
- Stop if the user cannot control the hinge or finish position.

### Kettlebell Romanian Deadlift

Include:

- Hinge setup.
- Bell close to the legs.
- Hamstring and hip control.
- Comfortable depth.
- Two-hand, staggered-stance, and single-leg variations.

### Kettlebell Lunge

Include:

- Stable rack, suitcase, or goblet position.
- Step control.
- Knee tracking.
- Balance.
- Reverse, walking, and supported variations.

### Kettlebell Sumo Deadlift

Include:

- Wider stance.
- Toes and knees tracking together.
- Bell position.
- Hip hinge and leg drive.
- Controlled lowering.
- Elevated-start and lighter-load variations.

### Kettlebell Single-Leg Deadlift

Include:

- Balance and hip hinge.
- Pelvis control.
- Load kept close.
- Small range as a valid starting point.
- Supported, kickstand, and contralateral variations.

### Pistol Squat

Treat this as advanced.

Include:

- Ankle, hip, strength, and balance demands.
- Controlled depth.
- Counterbalance and support regressions.
- Box pistol and assisted pistol variations.
- Do not imply it is necessary for general fitness.

### Jump Squat

Include:

- Squat landing mechanics.
- Quiet, controlled landing.
- Appropriate jump height.
- Resetting between repetitions.
- Bodyweight, countermovement, and reduced-depth variations.
- Stop if landing control deteriorates.

### Box Jumps

Include:

- Box-height selection.
- Takeoff and landing.
- Stable landing position.
- Stepping down as a lower-impact option.
- Avoiding a box so high that landing becomes a deep squat or requires unsafe scrambling.
- Low-box and pause variations.

### Broad Jumps

Include:

- Horizontal projection.
- Arm swing.
- Two-foot takeoff and landing.
- Landing control.
- Reset between repetitions.
- Shorter-distance and stick-landing regressions.

### Nordic Curl

Treat this as advanced eccentric hamstring work.

Include:

- Stable kneeling setup.
- Controlled forward lowering.
- Use of assistance.
- Avoiding uncontrolled collapse.
- Partial-range, band-assisted, and hamstring-curl regressions.

### Step-ups

Include:

- Box or step height.
- Whole-foot placement.
- Driving through the working leg.
- Controlled step-down.
- Avoiding excessive push from the trailing leg.
- Lower-step, supported, and loaded variations.

### Tuck Jump

Include:

- Preparation and landing.
- Compact knee lift without forcing range.
- Quiet landing.
- Reset between jumps.
- Lower-height and squat-jump regressions.

### Lateral Bound

Include:

- Side-to-side projection.
- Single-leg landing control.
- Hip and knee alignment.
- Stick and reset.
- Shorter-distance, supported, and repeated-bound progressions.
