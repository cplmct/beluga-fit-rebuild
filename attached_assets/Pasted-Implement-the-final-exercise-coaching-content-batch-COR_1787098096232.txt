## Exercise-specific requirements

### Plank

Include:

- Forearm or high-plank setup.
- Head-to-heel alignment.
- Trunk bracing.
- Breathing while maintaining position.
- Appropriate hold duration.
- Kneeling and elevated regressions.

### Side Plank

Include:

- Elbow or hand support.
- Shoulder stacked over support.
- Side-body and hip control.
- Avoiding sagging or excessive rotation.
- Kneeling and elevated variations.

### Crunches

Include:

- Controlled spinal flexion.
- Ribcage moving toward the pelvis.
- Avoiding forceful neck pulling.
- Controlled lowering.
- Reverse-crunch and stability-ball options.

### Bicycle Crunches

Include:

- Trunk position.
- Controlled opposite elbow-to-knee movement.
- Rotation from the trunk rather than pulling the neck.
- Slow and reduced-range options.

### Russian Twists

Use careful coaching language.

Include:

- Seated balance setup.
- Trunk rotation within a comfortable range.
- Feet-supported regression.
- Avoiding high-speed twisting or forcing range.
- Bodyweight and light-load variations.

### Leg Raises

Include:

- Supine setup.
- Pelvic and lower-back control.
- Controlled lowering.
- Bent-knee regression.
- Avoiding momentum.

### Hanging Leg Raises

Treat this as more advanced than floor leg raises.

Include:

- Grip and shoulder setup.
- Controlled pelvic movement.
- Bent-knee progression.
- Avoiding swinging.
- Captain’s-chair and supported regressions.

### Ab Wheel

Treat this as an advanced anti-extension exercise.

Include:

- Kneeling setup.
- Braced trunk.
- Controlled rollout distance.
- Avoiding lumbar sag.
- Short-range, wall-stop, and stability-ball regressions.

### Cable Crunches

Include:

- Kneeling or standing setup.
- Cable height.
- Trunk flexion rather than pulling only with the arms.
- Controlled return.
- Avoiding excessive load and hip movement.

### Mountain Climbers

Include:

- High-plank setup.
- Trunk stability.
- Controlled knee drive.
- Slow and fast versions.
- Avoiding excessive hip bouncing.
- Elevated regression.

### Kettlebell Turkish Get-Up

Treat this as a complex, advanced movement.

Include:

- Light-load practice.
- Sequenced transitions.
- Keeping the kettlebell stacked over the shoulder.
- Using a stable gaze and controlled movement.
- Practicing partial segments.
- No-load and shoe/bottoms-up practice only where appropriate.
- Recommend qualified instruction for users unfamiliar with the movement.

### Kettlebell Windmill

Include:

- Wide stance.
- Hip shift and hinge.
- Shoulder position.
- Controlled side-bending/hinge combination.
- Light loading.
- Range limited by control and mobility.
- Bodyweight and shortened-range regressions.

### Kettlebell Russian Twist

Include:

- Seated setup.
- Controlled trunk rotation.
- Light load.
- Feet-supported regression.
- Avoiding fast or forced twisting.

### Kettlebell Farmer's Carry

Include:

- Tall posture.
- Grip and shoulder position.
- Short controlled steps.
- Breathing under load.
- Suitcase-carry option and lighter-load regression.
- Avoiding trunk leaning.

### Kettlebell Around the World

Include:

- Stable stance.
- Controlled hand-to-hand transfer around the body.
- Trunk control.
- Light kettlebell.
- Slower learning pace.
- Stationary and reduced-range variations.

### L-Sit

Treat this as advanced.

Include:

- Hand support and shoulder depression.
- Straight or tucked-leg options.
- Trunk control.
- Scapular stability.
- Tuck sit and parallettes regressions.

### Dragon Flag

Treat this as advanced.

Include:

- Stable bench or support.
- Shoulder and upper-back contact.
- Rigid body control.
- Controlled lowering only within available strength.
- Bent-knee, eccentric, and reverse-crunch regressions.
- Do not imply it is necessary for general fitness.

### Hollow Body Hold

Include:

- Supine setup.
- Rib and pelvis control.
- Bent-knee regression.
- Arm and leg position scaling.
- Avoiding lower-back arching beyond control.

### Superman Hold

Use balanced language.

Include:

- Prone setup.
- Gentle arm and leg lift.
- Controlled range.
- Avoiding aggressive lumbar compression or forced height.
- Alternating-arm/leg and shorter-duration variations.

### Bear Crawl

Include:

- Hands-under-shoulders setup.
- Knees hovering as tolerated.
- Contralateral movement.
- Trunk and hip control.
- Forward, backward, and stationary variations.
- Elevated regression.

### Inchworm

Include:

- Standing-to-hands setup.
- Controlled walkout.
- Stable plank position.
- Hips returning under control.
- Short-range and elevated regressions.

### Burpee Pull-up

Treat this as advanced and conditioning-focused.

Include:

- Safe pull-up-bar height.
- Squat, plank, return, jump, and pull-up sequence.
- Scaling by removing the pull-up or using an assisted pull-up.
- Controlling fatigue and landing.
- Step-back and low-impact regressions.
- Do not prescribe unsafe speed at the expense of control.
