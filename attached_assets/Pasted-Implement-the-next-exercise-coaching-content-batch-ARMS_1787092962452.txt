## Exercise-specific requirements

### Barbell Curl

Include:

- Stable standing setup.
- Grip and wrist position.
- Keeping the upper arm relatively still.
- Controlled lowering.
- Avoiding excessive torso swing.
- EZ-bar and incline dumbbell variations.

### Dumbbell Curl

Include:

- Standing or seated setup.
- Alternating and simultaneous options.
- Supination as tolerated.
- Controlled lowering.
- Avoiding shoulder movement and momentum.

### Hammer Curl

Include:

- Neutral grip.
- Elbow position.
- Brachialis and forearm contribution without overclaiming isolation.
- Alternating and cross-body versions.
- Controlled loading.

### Preacher Curl

Include:

- Upper-arm support.
- Appropriate seat and pad setup.
- Controlled elbow extension.
- Avoiding forceful lockout.
- Dumbbell, EZ-bar, and cable variations.

### Cable Curl

Include:

- Stable cable stance.
- Handle and pulley-height options.
- Continuous resistance.
- Controlled return.
- Avoiding leaning and momentum.

### Tricep Dips

Treat this as distinct from Chest Dips.

Include:

- Upright torso option.
- Elbow path.
- Shoulder comfort.
- Controlled depth.
- Bench-dip and assisted-dip regressions.
- Explain that torso angle and setup change relative emphasis.

### Close-grip Bench Press

Include:

- A narrower but comfortable grip, not an excessively narrow grip.
- Wrist and elbow alignment.
- Controlled bar path.
- Chest, shoulder, and triceps contribution.
- Dumbbell and push-up variations.

### Tricep Pushdown

Include:

- Cable height and handle selection.
- Stable upper arms.
- Elbow extension.
- Controlled return.
- Rope and single-arm variations.
- Avoiding shoulder swinging.

### Overhead Tricep Extension

Include:

- Overhead setup.
- Rib and trunk control.
- Comfortable elbow position.
- Controlled descent.
- Dumbbell, cable, and unilateral variations.
- Modifying range if shoulder mobility is limited.

### Skull Crushers

Include:

- Stable bench setup.
- Elbow position.
- Controlled lowering toward a comfortable target.
- Avoiding excessive load.
- EZ-bar, dumbbell, and cable variations.
- Stop or modify for elbow discomfort.

### Kettlebell Curl

Include:

- Stable stance.
- Grip and wrist control.
- Keeping the upper arm relatively still.
- Controlled lowering.
- Alternating and rack-supported variations.

### Kettlebell Tricep Extension

Include:

- Safe overhead position.
- Two-hand and one-hand options.
- Trunk control.
- Comfortable elbow position.
- Controlled range and lighter loading.
